About Us

The main focus of FNAI Lab is to utilize and leverage artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) to solve problems in Foodinformatics, AI gastronomy, Recipe recommendation, Personalized diet prescription, Precision nutrition. In order to diversify and strengthen its arsenal, FNAI Lab also conducts research in other areas such as natural language processing (NLP), recommendation systems, and graph ML in order to uncover more-refined techniques to carry out its larger mission.

For more detailed explanations, check out our on-going research.
